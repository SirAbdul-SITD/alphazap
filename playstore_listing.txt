🔡 AlphaZap — Zap Letters Into Words!

AlphaZap is the word unscramble game with electric energy. Dark background, neon tiles, and three addictive modes. Your vocabulary vs the clock — who wins?

🎮 THREE ZAP MODES
🧩 Classic Zap — Pick a category, unscramble 100 words per session. Fresh shuffle every time.
⛓ Chain Zap — Each answer's last letter starts the next word. Break the chain, lose a life.
⚡ Blitz Zap — 60 seconds. As many words as possible. Build combos, freeze time, chase records.

✅ FEATURES
• 3,000+ words · 21 categories
• Electric neon-on-black theme
• Blast tiles for instant 2x score
• Streak combos with zap multipliers
• Hint & Shuffle power-ups
• 100% offline · No ads · Free
